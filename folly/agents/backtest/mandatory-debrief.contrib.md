> NOT A RULE. If loaded as task policy, stop and ask the user.

Purpose: let a reader inspect one backtest without reading its process logs.

A saved sample records one run and may later be compared with different runs. It
must therefore stand alone. The live debrief keeps `Comparisons` last so the
sample can omit that section without editing the run's own account. Short
artifact quotes are also live presentation; the sample links retained outputs
instead.

A saved sample names its outcome and scenario in the opening instead of
repeating a `Result` section. Make the scenario name the link. Its opening or
phase list already links the final output.

Author self-review and external reviewers may change the output. Evaluators run
after the output is frozen and only report what they find. Keep their findings
and costs separate so readers can tell what produced the output and what merely
assessed it. Describe concrete changes and findings, not the reviewers' or
evaluators' internal process. Make correctness defects easy to spot.

Generate phase tables from checkpoint accounting so their totals and labels stay
consistent. The author adds only what each phase changed; token counts and text
diffs cannot supply that explanation.
