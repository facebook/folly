# Prepare external feedback

Mark proposals outside the user's agreed task as `SCOPE_EXPANSION`. Without
approval, do not apply them or let them block completion.

Check the remaining findings against the candidate and classify them using the
finding classes in `{FA}/critic-iterate.md`.

Before accepting a finding that would change the artifact, verify its factual
claims. Escalate if allowed evidence cannot repair a material finding.

If a `MUST_TAKE` finding changes a proposed fix's behavior, invalidates a
fallback, or exposes a deciding correctness assumption, reapply
`{FA}/design-vetting.md` before editing. Pure presentation or citation changes
do not trigger this.

Proposed wording or versions are drafts to assess in the author critique, not
instructions to paste.

Record dispositions only in the accountability artifact; summarize
`SCOPE_EXPANSION` items in the final debrief.
