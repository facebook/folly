> NOT A RULE. If loaded as task policy, stop and ask the user.

Purpose: test whether a reader can explain the text without background sources.

The task supplies its only inputs. It reports comprehension gaps without
searching background sources, proposing missing project work, or treating
repeated jargon as understanding.

Do not take a successful cold read as evidence that the text does its job well.
This only shows understanding, not fitness-for-purpose.
